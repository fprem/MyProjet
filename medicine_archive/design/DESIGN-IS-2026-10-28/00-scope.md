# Scope

- **Audited:** nothing yet. This is a new Android app (Flutter), so the audit
  skips evidence and scoring and goes straight to a NEW verdict.
- **Primary user:** one person (or a family carer) keeping track of the
  medicines each family member has at home.
- **Primary task:** photograph a medicine box and get its name and quantity
  into the right person's list, with as few taps as possible.
- **Constraints:** Android, Flutter, Material 3. All data stays on the phone
  and is encrypted. Photos are deleted right after the text is read. Text is
  read on-device (no network).
- **References:** none given.
